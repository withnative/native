# Contributing to Native

Thanks for reading the source.

Native is developed in a private upstream, and this repository is a curated,
read-only snapshot of selected work. The ordinary open-source contribution flow
does not fit it yet — not because we want to keep people out, but because we are
a small team, moving quickly, and we would rather get to know someone than
collect patches from strangers.

**So contribution here happens by invitation, after a conversation.**

What that means in practice:

- **Please don't open unsolicited pull requests.** They are not a route into
  this repository, and we would rather not leave your work sitting unread.
- **GitHub Issues and Discussions are turned off here**, so this is not the
  place for bug reports or support questions either.
- **Start with a conversation instead.** [withnative.ai](https://www.withnative.ai/)
  carries current product information and ways to reach the team. Tell us what
  you are building, what you have found, or which part of this you care about.
  We read all of it.
- **If we do invite a contribution**, we will agree the scope with you first and
  ask you to sign a contributor licence agreement before anything is merged, so
  that the licensing of this repository stays clear for everyone relying on it.

This reflects where the project is rather than where it is going. The source is
published so that it can be inspected and trusted, and that is a different thing
from being jointly developed. As the work settles, we expect the balance to
move — and the people we have talked to along the way are the ones we will want
building it with us.
