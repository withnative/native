Keyset pager

Loading host input.

    A proof tab for the canonical keyset paging pattern. It reads the names of every record you can see in pages of 200, ordered by id, at most 25 page reads per load. Each page runs as you when it is read, so a page shows only records you could see at that moment, and a record you lose access to is left out of later pages. Rows already on screen stay until you read again. It cannot change anything.
